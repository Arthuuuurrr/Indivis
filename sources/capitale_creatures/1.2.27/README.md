# Capitale Creatures 1.2.27 / Biomes BETA 0.21

Active aggressive-pool rebalance.

## Changes
- Natural-spawn dinos keep HP and armor unchanged; attack damage is reduced to a tank-oriented curve.
- Dungeon orcs are slightly reduced: lower spawn weights and local cap 24 -> 20.
- Recent Lands of Icaria corruption additions are restricted to indivis:corruption and removed from capitale:donjon.
- Small Empire hostiles receive higher weights to reduce dinosaur dominance.
- Aquatic AI remains OPEN: 3D swimming/navigation, target acquisition/pursuit and water/land behavior still require dedicated runtime validation.
